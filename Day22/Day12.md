Day 22 - Startup Idea Validation with Claude

Objective

Validate a startup idea using AI-powered market research and startup analysis.

Startup Idea

Student Opportunity Network (SON)

Problem Statement

Students face difficulty finding internships, hackathons, scholarships, certifications, and career opportunities because information is scattered across multiple platforms.

Target Audience

- College Students
- Freshers
- Aspiring Software Developers
- Job Seekers

Activities Performed

- Problem Validation
- Founder-Market Fit Analysis
- TAM, SAM, SOM Analysis
- Competitor Analysis
- Market Gap Analysis
- Customer Persona Creation
- Risk Assessment
- Go / No-Go Recommendation
- 30-Day Action Plan

Key Insights

- Strong demand exists for centralized student opportunities.
- Existing solutions are fragmented.
- Community-driven approach can provide differentiation.
- Initial validation should focus on student communities and colleges.

Deliverables

- Startup Validation Report
- Founder-Market Fit Score
- TAM/SAM/SOM Analysis
- Competitor Analysis
- Market Gap Analysis
- Go / No-Go Recommendation
- 30-Day Action Plan

Learnings

- Importance of validating ideas before development.
- Understanding market size and competition.
- Identifying customer pain points.
- Creating actionable startup roadmaps.
