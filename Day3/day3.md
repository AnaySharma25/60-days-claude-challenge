Day3 - Role-Based Prompting

What is Role-Based Prompting?

Role-Based Prompting means assigning a specific role or persona to the AI before asking a question. This helps the AI respond from the perspective of an expert in that field.

Why It Matters

Role-Based Prompting improves the quality, relevance, and depth of AI responses.

Prompt Used Without Role

How can I grow my digital agency and get my first 5 clients?

Observation

The answer was general and broad.

---

Prompt Used With Founder Persona

Act as a successful startup founder who has built multiple profitable agencies.

How can I grow my digital agency and get my first 5 clients?

Observation

The answer focused on business growth, client acquisition, networking, and strategy.

---

Prompt Used With Developer Persona

Act as a senior software developer and tech entrepreneur.

How can I grow my digital agency and get my first 5 clients?

Observation

The answer focused on portfolio building, technical credibility, websites, and project delivery.

---

Key Learnings

1. Different roles generate different perspectives.
2. Role prompts create more specialized answers.
3. Responses become more practical and relevant.
4. AI can simulate real-world experts.
5. Role-Based Prompting improves decision-making.

Conclusion

Today I learned that assigning a role to AI significantly changes the quality and focus of responses. Different personas provide different insights for the same question.
