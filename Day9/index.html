Medical Disclaimer

NutriScope is an educational tool, not a medical device.

• Nutrient values are approximate and based on standard food composition databases. Actual values vary by preparation method, cooking, soil quality, and food variety.

• Dietary Reference Intakes (DRIs) used are based on ICMR 2020 guidelines for Indian adults and WHO recommendations. Individual needs differ based on health conditions, medications, and genetics.

• Do not use this application to diagnose, treat, or manage any medical condition. Always consult a Registered Dietitian (RD) or qualified healthcare provider for personalised dietary advice.

• Risk flags in the Risk Analysis tab indicate nutritional patterns, not clinical diagnoses. Seek professional evaluation if you have concerns.

• The creators of NutriScope accept no liability for decisions made based on this application's output.
