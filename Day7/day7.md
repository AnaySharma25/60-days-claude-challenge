Day 7 - Claude Model Selection & Reasoning Effort
Objective
Learn how to choose the right Claude model and reasoning effort for different tasks.
Key Learnings
Why Model Selection Matters
Different Claude models are optimized for different use cases:
1- Fast Tasks → Quick responses and simple queries
2- Balanced Tasks → Everyday work and content creation
3- Advanced Tasks → Complex reasoning, coding, and analysis
Reasoning Effort Levels
Low Reasoning
Best for:
* Summaries
* Simple Q&A
* Basic writing
Medium Reasoning
Best for:
* Research
* Planning
* Content creation
* Learning roadmaps
High Reasoning
Best for:
* Coding projects
* Business strategy
* Complex problem solving
* Multi-step analysis
My Personalized Claude Strategy
Daily Learning
* Model: Balanced
* Reasoning: Medium
* Coding Projects
* Model: Advanced
* Reasoning: High
Resume & Career Guidance
* Model: Advanced
* Reasoning: High
Content Creation
* Model: Balanced
* Reasoning: Medium
Quick Questions
* Model: Fast
* Reasoning: Low
Key Insight
Choosing the correct model before writing the prompt improves response quality, accuracy, and efficiency.
Outcome
Learned how to match Claude models and reasoning effort with specific tasks for better AI productivity.
