Day 23 – Customer & MVP Blueprint

Objective

Created a Customer & MVP Blueprint using AI to identify ideal customers, customer pain points, MVP features, pricing strategy, and product-market fit potential.

Key Deliverables

- Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- Buyer Persona
- Top 10 Customer Pain Points
- Customer Journey Mapping
- Buying Triggers & Objections
- MVP Recommendation
- MoSCoW Prioritization
- Pricing Hypothesis
- Top Risks Assessment
- 30-Day MVP Plan
- Founder Action Sheet

Key Learnings

- Customer research is critical before building a product.
- MVP should focus on solving one core problem.
- Understanding customer objections helps improve conversion rates.
- Prioritization frameworks like MoSCoW reduce development waste.
- Early validation reduces startup risk.

Outcome

Generated a complete Customer & MVP Blueprint report and identified actionable next steps for product development and validation.

Tools Used

- Claude AI
- GitHub
- Markdown
